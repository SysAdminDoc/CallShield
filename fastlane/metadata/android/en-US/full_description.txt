CallShield blocks spam calls and text messages with layered on-device
detection, scheduled public spam-data updates, and a privacy-first Android
experience.

Core protection runs locally on your phone. CallShield combines personal
allow/block rules, contacts, public spam databases, prefix and wildcard rules,
quiet hours, callback detection, STIR/SHAKEN signals, heuristics, campaign
burst detection, SMS keyword analysis, and a pure Kotlin machine-learning
scorer.

Highlights:

* 15+ detection layers for calls, SMS, and RCS notification spam
* On-device spam scoring with no required account, ads, analytics, or tracking
* Public spam database and hot-list sync from the open source repository
* Real-time caller ID overlay for suspicious calls
* User-controlled blocklist, whitelist, wildcard, range, and keyword rules
* Backup/restore and CSV export for local data portability
* Optional community spam reports through the project's Cloudflare Worker
* Caller lookup sources require no account or API key
* Android 10+ support with Material 3 and accessibility-focused touch targets

Network access is limited to public spam-data sync, optional caller lookup,
optional URL safety checks, and optional anonymous community reports. No Google
Play Services, Firebase, Crashlytics, advertising SDK, or analytics SDK is
required.

Mendeley SMS phishing set (mendeley-sms.tsv)

mendeley-sms.tsv holds 5,831 texts from the dataset published as:

  Sandhya Mishra and Devpriya Soni. SMS Phishing Dataset for Machine
  Learning and Pattern Recognition. Mendeley Data, V1, 2022.
  https://doi.org/10.17632/f45bkkt8pr.1

Source: https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/f45bkkt8pr/1

Changes: scripts/sample_mendeley_sms_corpus.py kept three fields (row number,
label and message text) and one row per distinct text, trimmed of spaces and
tabs at either end. Labels were folded to lower case, and the 34 texts filed
as both smishing and spam kept the smishing label. HTML entities such as
&lt;#&gt; were decoded. In clean texts, runs of seven or more digits became 555
numbers of the same length, since those can be people's own numbers. Nothing
else in the text was changed. SmsEvaluationCorpusTest swaps the placeholders
the source already had, such as <#> and <TIME>, for neutral stand-ins when it
reads a row.

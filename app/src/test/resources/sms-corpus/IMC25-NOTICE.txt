IMC 2025 smishing sample (imc25-sample.tsv)

imc25-sample.tsv holds 5,000 rows drawn from the dataset published with:

  Sharad Agarwal, Antonis Papasavva, Guillermo Suarez-Tangil and Marie Vasek.
  Fishing for Smishing: Understanding SMS Phishing Infrastructure and
  Strategies by Mining Public User Reports. Proceedings of the 2025 ACM
  Internet Measurement Conference (IMC '25).
  https://doi.org/10.1145/3730567.3764431

Source: https://github.com/reportsmishing/Smishing-Dataset-IMC25

Changes: scripts/sample_imc25_corpus.py dropped duplicate texts and kept every
wrong-number and hey mum/dad report. It then sampled the rest by language, with
a floor so small languages have enough rows to measure. Only six fields were kept:
row number, language (as a BCP 47 tag), scam type, sender type, URL shortener
and message text. The text itself is unchanged. The authors had already replaced
links, phone numbers and names with placeholders such as <URL>, and
SmsEvaluationCorpusTest swaps those for neutral stand-ins when it reads a row.
